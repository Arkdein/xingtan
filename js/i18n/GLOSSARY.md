Keep these terms consistent. The tool names and common buttons are already in
`zh-core.js`, which takes priority over the group files.

Style:
- Short and direct, like a well-made Chinese app: 保存, not 请保存.
- No “请” on buttons. Use it sparingly in instructions to students.
- Full-width punctuation in Chinese: ，。：；？！（） and “ ”.
- Keep numbers and placeholders as they are: `{n}`, `{name}`.
- Keep product names in English: YouTube, Google Forms, PDF, CSV, QR.
